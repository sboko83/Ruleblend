# Code of Conduct

[Русская версия](docs/ru/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Expected behavior

Treat participants respectfully, regardless of background, identity or experience. Discuss the
technical problem, give concrete feedback and accept reasonable disagreement. Respect privacy
and use synthetic examples when discussing user files or assistant configurations.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, sharing another person's private
information without consent, and persistent disruption are unacceptable.

These rules apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces, and when
someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [s.bokonyaev@yandex.ru](mailto:s.bokonyaev@yandex.ru) with the
subject `Ruleblend conduct`. Include relevant context and links; avoid sharing unrelated personal
information. Vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer reviews reports, limits disclosure of private details to what handling the report
requires, and may remove content, issue a warning or restrict participation according to the
severity and repetition of the behavior. Participants may ask for clarification or reconsideration
through the same private channel.
